More Than 1 & Special Powers!

🐱🐱🐱

More Than 1 (Plural)

When there are 2 or more people/animals/things, Sally gets scared of the crowd and runs away!

The cats run. (No "s")

Special "I" and "YOU"

I and YOU have special superhero powers! Sally is scared of them and runs away!

I run. / You sleep. (No "s")

Question: Choose the correct verb for the sentence below.

"The two frogs ______ into the pond."

💡 Socratic Hint:

How many frogs are there? Is there 1 frog, or more than 1 frog? Does Sally get scared of crowds?

✅ Step-by-Step Explanation:

Step 1: Look at the subject $\rightarrow$ "two frogs".

Step 2: "two frogs" means MORE THAN ONE frog (Plural).

Step 3: Sally the Snake gets scared of crowds and runs away!

Step 4: No "s" is needed at the end of the verb.

Correct Answer: jump

📋 Sally the Snake Rule Summary:

1. Only 1 Person / Animal / Thing (Singular): Sally attaches to the verb! Add s.
Example: The dog barks.
2. More Than 1 (Plural): Sally gets scared and runs away! NO s.
Example: The dogs bark.
3. Special Powers ("I" and "YOU"): Sally is scared of superhero "I" and "You"! NO s.
Example: I bark. / You bark.
