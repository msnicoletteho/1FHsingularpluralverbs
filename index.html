Story Time & Grammar Rules

Meet Sally the Snake! 🐍

Sally loves grammar! When there is only one person, thing, or animal, Sally attacks (the verb gets an ‘s’!). When there are many, or when you use "I" or "You", Sally runs away!

Rule #1
🐱💤

Only ONE (Singular)

When the subject is singular (just 1), Sally attacks! We add an ‘s’ to the action verb.

Example:
The cat sleeps.
1 cat = add 's'!
Rule #2
🐈🐈💨

More than ONE (Plural)

When there are 2 or more subjects, Sally runs away! The action verb does NOT get an ‘s’.

Example:
The cats sleep.
2+ cats = NO 's'!
Superpower Rule
🦸‍♂️✨

"I" & "You" Superpowers!

Even though "I" and "You" are singular, they have superpowers! Sally runs away from them too. No ‘s’!

Examples:
I run. | You jump.
Special rule for I and You!
